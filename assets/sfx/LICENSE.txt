Bundled SFX Pack — License & Sources
=====================================

These audio files are synthetic placeholders generated locally with ffmpeg
(lavfi noise/tone sources). They are intended for development, CI, and offline
pipeline testing only. They are not representative of production-quality sound
design.

For production polish, replace files under assets/sfx/ with CC0-licensed
recordings from reputable libraries, for example:

- CC0 Sounds (https://cc0sounds.com/) — weather, nature, and urban ambience
- Signature Sounds CC0 weather packs — rain, wind, thunder loops
- Freesound.org (filter by CC0 license) — footsteps, doors, crowd reactions
- OpenGameArt.org CC0 audio section — short UI/whoosh-style cues

When substituting assets, keep filenames and folder layout unchanged so the
pipeline tag map continues to resolve correctly. Document any third-party
attribution requirements in this file if a chosen license differs from CC0.
